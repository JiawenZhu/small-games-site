Six small games you can actually finish.

Five of them open inside TikTok with nothing to download — a whole game in about 130 kilobytes, which is less than the photo you just scrolled past. One of them is a 3D rescue adventure on the App Store. The phone on this page is not a picture: it is the real game, running now.

How these are made

The five TikTok games share one runtime and one design system: a Canvas2D engine written from scratch, no framework, no dependencies. Each one ships as its own package of around 130 KB. Save Doge runs a real rigid-body solver on the line you draw; Nyandoku's fifty boards are generated and then machine-proven to have exactly one solution each.

Bunny Escape is the odd one out — twenty 3D levels, built in the browser and shipped to iOS, with its own models, music and story.
