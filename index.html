About Quad Squirrel

We make small, handcrafted games designed to fit into little pockets of your day. Every puzzle opens instantly, runs smoothly, and respects your time.

The name comes from the squirrels on the Main Quad at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign — chubby, fearless, and always figuring out the best route.
